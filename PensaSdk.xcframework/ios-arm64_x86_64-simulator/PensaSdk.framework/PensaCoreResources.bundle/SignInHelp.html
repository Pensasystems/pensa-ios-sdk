Single Sign-On (SSO) FAQs

Common questions about implementation, security and troubleshooting.

🛠️

Troubleshooting & Usage

Why do I have to enter my full email address?

The app uses your email domain to know which organization to redirect you to.

What if a user can't log in?
  1. Test the IdP. Have the user try logging into your company portal directly.
  2. Check configuration. If they can log into your portal but not Pensa, contact us.
  3. Support. Reach out to your internal IT team first, or submit a support ticket in the app.
⚙️

Setup & Prerequisites

What are the requirements to get started?
  1. A Pensa-configured onboarding structure.
  2. An exchange of SAML Metadata (SP and IdP).
  3. Designated user groups and account assignments within Pensa.
Is there any downtime during implementation?

No. SSO can be configured and tested without any interruption to your current service.

Can I restrict access to certain groups or IPs?

Yes. Since authentication happens on your side, you can apply your own conditional access policies — such as IP ranges or specific user groups — within your IdP.

🛡️

Security & Authentication

Which protocols do you support?

We support SAML 2.0 for all SSO integrations.

Which Identity Providers (IdPs) are compatible?

Any IdP that supports the SAML 2.0 standard, for example:

Okta Azure AD / Entra ID Ping …and others
Does this support Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)?

Yes. We delegate authentication to your IdP. If you have MFA enabled there, it automatically applies to Pensa.

How is my data protected?

We only sync essential user data:

  • Name
  • Surname
  • Email
  • Username
How long do sessions last?

Once logged in via SSO, sessions remain active for 30 days.
